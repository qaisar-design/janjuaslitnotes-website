Janjua’s English Lit Notes: Privacy and legal notices

These privacy and legal notices explain what this site collects (nothing by itself: no cookies and no tracking), how an email you send is used, and the legal facts about the books: copyright, the public-domain texts, quotations and the use of AI.

Privacy and legal notices at a glance

  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no sign-up.
  • An email you send is used only for what you asked, and never added to a list, sold or shared.
  • The original texts are in the public domain; everything else in the books is the author’s own work.
  • AI tools were used in preparing the books, and every quotation, line number, fact and answer was then checked.
  • An independent study aid, not linked to any university, examination board or publisher.

Privacy

Who is responsible for your information?

Qaisar Iqbal Janjua, Ontario, Canada, the author of Janjua’s English Lit Notes, is responsible for any personal information sent to him. Write to qaisar@janjuaslitnotes.com with any question or complaint about privacy.

What does this site collect?

Nothing by itself. There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no sign-up, and the free samples download without asking for any details. The fonts and images are served from this site, so no font service sees your visit. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which records visitors’ IP addresses for security (GitHub’s note on GitHub Pages).

What happens when you send an email?

The buttons and forms on this site only open your own email app with a message written out. Nothing is sent until you press Send there. You then send your email address, your name if you give it, and what you write. The mailbox is hosted by Microsoft 365.

How is your email used?

Only to answer you, to check and list an error you report, or, if you asked to hear when a book is out, to send you one email when that book is out. Your address is never added to a mailing list, sold or shared.

How long is it kept?

A request to hear when a book is out is deleted once that email has been sent. Other emails are kept only as long as needed to deal with them, and no longer than two years.

What are your rights?

You can ask to see, correct or delete what is held about you, or withdraw a request, by writing to qaisar@janjuaslitnotes.com. For readers in the European Union and the United Kingdom, the legal basis is a legitimate interest in answering a message you chose to send. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or to the data protection authority where you live.

What if you are under 16?

Please ask a parent or guardian to send the request for you. In India, ask a parent or guardian if you are under 18.

Do other sites follow this notice?

No. Links to other sites, such as Amazon once the books are on sale, take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

Legal notices

Who owns the content of the books and this site?

© 2026 Qaisar Iqbal Janjua. The modern English lines, word notes, answers, essays, questions, drawings, covers and the text of this website are his original work and are protected by copyright. Short quotations for study or review are welcome with credit; please ask before copying anything more.

Which texts are in the public domain?

The original works are in the public domain. The books print the text of Skeat’s edition of Chaucer (Clarendon Press, Oxford, 1900) and of the Cambridge Shakespeare, edited by W. G. Clark and W. A. Wright (Macmillan, 1865 and 1866), with obvious misprints corrected.

How are critics quoted?

Briefly, for criticism and study, always with the author, title, publisher and year, and always followed by the book’s own comment.

Was AI used to make the books?

Yes. The author used AI tools for research, drafting, the modern English lines and the drawings, including the covers. Every quotation and line number was then checked against the book’s text, and every fact and answer against published sources, before publication. Each book says so on its copyright page.

Are the books official or endorsed by an exam board?

No. Janjua’s English Lit Notes is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any university, examination board or publisher. The practice questions are written for this series in the forms examiners commonly use, and no exam paper is reproduced.

Whose trademarks are named here?

Amazon and Kindle are trademarks of Amazon.com, Inc. or its affiliates. Other names of products, publishers and services belong to their owners and are used only to identify them.

Which fonts does the site use?

Gloock, Instrument Sans, Source Sans 3 and Source Serif 4, served from this site under the SIL Open Font License.

What if you find an error?

Every confirmed error is listed on the corrections page and fixed in the next printing.
