Questions and answers

About the free samples

What you get, and what the full books add.

Are the samples really free?

Yes. All three samples download directly as PDF files, with no sign-up and no email needed, and the Macbeth sample unit can also be read online, as a web page.

What is in each free sample?

Each sample has the cover, the title page, A Note from the Author, the full contents and one complete unit, exactly as it appears in the book. The General Prologue sample gives Unit 15, the Wife of Bath; the Hamlet sample gives Unit 8, Act 3, Scene 1 with the “To be, or not to be” soliloquy; the Macbeth sample gives Unit 5, Act 2, Scenes 1 and 2, with the dagger soliloquy and the murder of Duncan.

What does the full book add?

The full General Prologue book has 24 units, twelve model essays and 180 objective questions; the full Hamlet book has 17 units, twelve model essays and 145 objective questions; the full Macbeth book has 13 units, twelve model essays and 105 objective questions. All three add practice questions, one-line facts and a last-night checklist, with a quotation bank in the first two and a bank of forty key passages in Macbeth.
