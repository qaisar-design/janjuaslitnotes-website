Who is Qaisar Iqbal Janjua?

Qaisar Iqbal Janjua has written notes for literature students for many years. He is now relaunching his series under a new name, Janjua’s English Lit Notes, one book at a time, and every book is rebuilt from the original text.

His aim is answers, not long biographies or commentary. A student with an exam coming needs to know what is happening, what the characters mean, and how to write about it well. Everything in his books is there for that.

What is Janjua’s English Lit Notes?

Janjua’s English Lit Notes is a series of exam study editions of the English literature works taught worldwide, for students in schools, colleges and universities wherever English literature is taught. Every book is built the same way:

  • The complete text, with plain modern English for every line of the original and word notes beneath.
  • Units by section or scene, each with a character or scene card, a background box, a critical note, three short answers, a model context answer and five objective questions.
  • Twelve model essays on the questions examiners set most often, each with a marked shorter version.
  • Practice questions, one-line facts, a bank of key quotations or passages and a last-night checklist.
  • Original drawings of every character, in one house style.

Every quotation and line number is checked against the source edition. The first three books are the General Prologue study guide, the Hamlet study guide and the Macbeth study guide, with more titles planned.
